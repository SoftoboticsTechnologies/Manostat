Company overview

One of the leading organisations specialised in manpower supply.

Manostat Talent Solutions Pvt. Ltd. provides businesses with the right talent to meet their staffing needs. Finding the right employees can be a daunting task, that is why we are here to help.

Our team of experienced recruiters uses a comprehensive approach to identify, screen and select top-quality candidates who possess the skills, experience and attitude that match your company's culture. We work closely with clients to understand their unique needs and tailor the recruitment process accordingly.

Our story

We are pleased to introduce ourselves as one of the leading organisations specialised in the field of manpower supply, registered as a Commercial Establishment under the Karnataka Shops and Commercial Act, 1961. We strictly follow all statutory requirements including ESI and PF benefits under Labour Laws.

With our industry expertise, we understand that companies today require more than a skilled candidate. They need employees who are productive, exhibit a positive attitude, and have the ability to become a true member of their team.

Our vision

To lead in the creation and delivery of innovative workforce solutions and services that enable our clients to win in the changing world of work.

Our mission

To provide clients with the best workforce solutions by delivering high-quality candidates with the necessary skills and expertise, while empowering individuals and building long-term relationships based on trust, integrity and mutual respect.
